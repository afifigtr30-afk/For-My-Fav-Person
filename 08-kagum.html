One thing that I really admire about you is the way you are. You ada hati yang baik, and you never stop trying walaupun I tahu you penat. Cara you hadap semua benda dengan tenang buat I respect you even more. Dengan you, I always rasa tenang and safe. I am really grateful that I get to love someone as kind and amazing as you. I hope you never forget how special you are to me. 🤍
