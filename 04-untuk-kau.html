For You, My Special One

Sayang, it is never just today that you are special to me, because you are special every single day. 🤍 Tak kisah sejauh mana pun jarak antara kita, you always know how to make me feel calm and happy. Setiap kali dapat message daripada you, mesti I senyum. Thank you sebab selalu ada untuk I, selalu sabar dengan I, and loving I the way you do. You are my safe place, my comfort, and the only person that truly feels like home. I cannot wait for the day we do not have to be apart anymore. Until then, just remember that I will always choose you. I love you so much. 🤍
